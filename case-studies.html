Healthcare

Multi-Speciality Hospital: Owning "Near Me" Searches

The Challenge

A well-equipped hospital was losing patients to smaller clinics simply because it didn't show up when people searched "hospital near me" or speciality keywords. Walk-ins were steady, but digital enquiries were almost zero.

What We Did

  • Rebuilt and verified the Google Business Profile with speciality-wise services, photos and posts
  • Launched a review-generation system at discharge — turning happy patients into 5-star proof
  • Department-wise local SEO pages + appointment funnel with click-to-WhatsApp
  • IVR system so every enquiry call was answered, routed and recorded

CBSE School: Filling Seats in One Admission Season

The Challenge

A reputed school with excellent infrastructure was invisible online. Parents researching schools found competitors first — and admissions plateaued despite word-of-mouth goodwill.

What We Did

  • Campus content shoots: facility reels, teacher spotlights, student achievements
  • Parent-targeted Meta ads by locality, with an admission enquiry lead form
  • WhatsApp follow-up sequence for every enquiry — brochure, fee structure, campus visit booking
