How It Works

Weights, Boxes And Ordering

01

Sold by weight

Most sweets come in 250g, 500g and 1kg. Mixed boxes are made up to whatever weight you ask for.

02

Taste first

Ask at the counter. It is normal, and it is the fastest way to settle an argument about which burfi to take.

03

Large orders

For weddings, offices and festivals, give us a day or two. Tell us the occasion and roughly the number of people.
