A metal fabrication and manufacturing company, working the same line since 2012

Dev Engineering manufactures material handling and storage equipment, and takes up metal fabrication, machining and toolroom work against customer requirements.

Company Background

Established in 2012

Dev Engineering is engaged in metal fabrication, industrial material handling equipment and manufacturing solutions for industrial customers.

With its head office at IMT Manesar, Gurgaon, the company manufactures industrial trolleys, pallets, bins, storage racks and other fabricated products for factory and material handling applications.

In 2019, Dev Engineering expanded its operations with a second manufacturing unit in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

The company's manufacturing capabilities cover metal fabrication, CNC and VMC machining, laser cutting, bending, welding, milling, drilling and tapping.

Along with its standard product range, Dev Engineering undertakes fabrication work based on customer drawings and specific industrial requirements.

The focus remains on practical manufacturing, accurate fabrication, consistent finishing and dependable service.

Standard designs are available across all five product groups. Where a standard design does not fit, the product is built to your drawing or to a sample.

How an Enquiry Runs

From your requirement to delivery

STEP 01

Requirement

Send a drawing, a sample, a photograph of the part, or simply tell us what has to be moved or stored and where.

STEP 02

Design & costing

The construction and sizes are worked out against that requirement, and a quotation goes back to you.

STEP 03

Fabrication

The job is fabricated in our own unit, with toolroom operations done in-house where the part needs them.

STEP 04

Finishing & delivery

Finishing is checked before dispatch, and delivery is made against the date agreed at the order stage.
