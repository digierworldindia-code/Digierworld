An industrial fabrication company, working the same line since 2012

Dev Engineering Works manufactures material handling and storage equipment, and takes up MS fabrication and toolroom work against customer requirements.

Company Background

Started in Gurgaon in 2012

The company began with MS fabrication and industrial trolley work for factories in and around Gurgaon. That is still the core of the business.

A second unit followed in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, in 2019, giving the company a base in the west alongside the original works in Haryana.

What leaves the shop today falls into two groups. The first is our own product range — material movement trolleys, MS pallets and bins, metal storage rack systems, office storage systems and UPS battery racks. The second is fabrication and toolroom work taken up against a customer's drawing, where the requirement is specific to a part, a fixture or a spot on the shop floor.

Most of what we build is not off the shelf. A trolley that carries a machined casting is not the same as one that carries painted panels, and a rack built for dies is not the same as a rack built for cartons. The sizes and construction get set by what is actually being handled.

Standard designs are available across all five product groups. Where a standard design does not fit, the product is built to your drawing or to a sample.

How an Enquiry Runs

From your requirement to delivery

STEP 01

Requirement

Send a drawing, a sample, a photograph of the part, or simply tell us what has to be moved or stored and where.

STEP 02

Design & costing

The construction and sizes are worked out against that requirement, and a quotation goes back to you.

STEP 03

Fabrication

The job is fabricated in our own unit, with toolroom operations done in-house where the part needs them.

STEP 04

Finishing & delivery

Finishing is checked before dispatch, and delivery is made against the date agreed at the order stage.
