The Company

Metal fabrication and material handling, from two units

Established in 2012, Dev Engineering is engaged in metal fabrication, industrial material handling equipment and manufacturing solutions for industrial customers.

From its head office at IMT Manesar, Gurgaon, the company manufactures industrial trolleys, pallets, bins, storage racks and other fabricated products for factory and material handling applications. A second manufacturing unit in Ahmedabad, Gujarat has been operational since 2019.

Manufacturing capabilities cover metal fabrication, CNC and VMC machining, laser cutting, bending, welding, milling, drilling and tapping. Alongside the standard product range, fabrication work is taken up against customer drawings.

In-House Manufacturing

Metal fabrication & toolroom facilities in-house

Machining, cutting, forming and welding are done in our own units, which keeps the job moving and keeps control over the finish.

CNC & VMC Machining

Machining centres in-house, for our own fabricated products and for customer job work.

Laser Cutting

Sheet and plate cut to profile for industrial components and fabricated assemblies.

Bending

Sheet and section bending as part of the fabrication process, not bought in.

Welding & Fabrication as per Drawing

Welding and fabrication against customer-provided drawings and requirements.

Milling, Drilling & Tapping

Milling, drilling and tapping on the toolroom machines, plus slot cutting, reaming, burnishing and boring.

Die Fixtures & SPM

Die fixtures and special purpose machines built around the component and the operation.
