The Company

MS fabrication and material handling, from two units

Dev Engineering Works started in Gurgaon in 2012, doing MS fabrication and manufacturing industrial trolleys. A second unit followed in Ahmedabad in 2019.

The work splits into two halves. One is our own product range — trolleys, pallets, bins, storage racks, office storage and battery racks. The other is fabrication and toolroom work taken up against customer drawings, where the requirement is specific to a part, a fixture or a location on the floor.

Capabilities

Toolroom facilities in-house

Fabrication on its own only takes a job so far. Holes have to be placed, slots cut, bores finished. Having those operations in the same premises keeps the work moving and keeps control over the finish.
