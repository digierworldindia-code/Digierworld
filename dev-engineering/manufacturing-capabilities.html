Manufacturing & Fabrication Capabilities

MS fabrication and industrial trolley manufacturing, backed by toolroom operations in-house. Work is taken up against standard designs, your drawings, or a sample part.

Fabrication

MS fabrication, built around the job

Fabrication in mild steel is the base of everything we make — trolleys, pallets, bins, racks, almirahs and battery systems all come out of the same shop.

The same capability is available as job work. Where a customer has a drawing for a frame, a fixture, a structure or a handling attachment, we quote and build against it. Where there is no drawing, a sample or the part itself is usually enough to work from.

Stainless steel is used where the application calls for it — SS trolleys and SS pallets are part of the standard range.

Toolroom Facilities

Machining operations in-house

Holes have to be placed, slots cut and bores finished. Keeping these operations in the same premises as the fabrication keeps the job moving and keeps control over the finish.

Toolroom

Machining alongside the fabrication shop

Toolroom work runs on both fronts — finishing operations on parts we fabricate ourselves, and machining on automotive components sent in by customers.
