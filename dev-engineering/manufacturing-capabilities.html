Manufacturing & Fabrication Capabilities

Metal fabrication, CNC and VMC machining, laser cutting, bending and welding, with toolroom operations in-house. Work is taken up against standard designs, your drawings, or a sample part.

Fabrication

Metal fabrication, built around the job

Metal fabrication is the base of everything we make — trolleys, pallets, bins, racks, almirahs and battery systems all come out of the same shop.

The same capability is available as job work. Where a customer has a drawing for a frame, a fixture, a structure or a handling attachment, we quote and build against it. Where there is no drawing, a sample or the part itself is usually enough to work from.

Stainless steel is used where the application calls for it — SS trolleys and SS pallets are part of the standard range.

In-House Facilities

Metal fabrication & toolroom facilities in-house

Five confirmed capability groups, run in our own units rather than subcontracted out.

CNC & VMC Machining

CNC and VMC machining centres in-house, used both for our own fabricated products and for machining work sent in by customers.

Laser Cutting

Laser cutting of sheet and plate for industrial metal components and fabricated assemblies, cut to profile from your drawing.

Bending

Sheet and section bending as part of the fabrication process, so formed parts are made in-house rather than bought in.

Welding & Fabrication as per Drawing

Welding and fabrication taken up against customer-provided drawings and specific industrial requirements.

Milling, Drilling & Tapping

Milling, drilling and tapping carried out on the toolroom machines, on fabricated assemblies and on individual components.

Toolroom Operations

Machining operations, listed individually

Holes have to be placed, slots cut and bores finished. Keeping these operations in the same premises as the fabrication keeps the job moving and keeps control over the finish.

01

Milling

Milling operations on fabricated assemblies and individual components.

02

Drilling

Hole drilling on fabricated and machined parts.

03

Tapping

Thread tapping, carried out alongside drilling on the same parts.

04

Slot Cutting

Slot cutting on components that need keyways and located openings.

05

Profile Cutting

Cutting components to profile from sheet and section.

06

Reaming

Reaming to bring drilled holes to a finished size and surface.

07

Burnishing

Burnishing to finish bore surfaces.

08

Boring

Boring on components that need larger, accurately located holes.

Engineering & Special Fabrication

Die fixtures, SPM and component work

Alongside the standard product range, these are built to the operation they are meant to serve.

Die Fixtures

Fixtures built to hold and locate a component through a machining or assembly operation, made to the part and the process.

Special Purpose Machines (SPM)

Purpose-built machines assembled around a specific operation, where a standard machine does not suit the job.

Automotive Component Work

Machining work on automotive components, run alongside the fabrication side of the business.

Die fixtures and special purpose machines are built to the customer's component and process. Send the part drawing and the operation it has to perform, and we will work out the build with you.
