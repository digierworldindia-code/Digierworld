Industries & Customers

Material handling and storage equipment goes wherever parts move and stock sits. These are the areas the product range is built around, and some of the companies we have supplied.

Where Our Products Go

Industries we supply

01

Automotive & Auto Components

Component and sub-assembly manufacturers who move parts between processes all day. Trolleys, bins and part-specific handling equipment make up most of this work, along with machining on automotive components.

02

UPS & Power Backup

Open and cabinet type racks for UPS battery banks, built around the cell layout the installation needs.

03

General Engineering & Manufacturing

Fabricated equipment and job work for engineering units — racks, pallets, bins, structures and parts made to drawing.

04

Warehousing & Stores

Storage rack systems for finished goods, spares and raw material, including slotted angle shelving, heavy duty bays and FIFO racking.

05

Tool Rooms & Die Storage

Racks built for dies and tooling, with compartment sizes set by what is being stored and how it is lifted in and out.

06

Offices & Record Rooms

Steel almirahs, file racks and movable ladders for the office and store side of the same plants.

Working With Us

What a first order usually looks like

Most enquiries start with one item — a trolley for a particular part, a rack for a store, a set of bins for a line. Sample or first-lot approval comes before a repeat order is placed, which is the sensible way round for equipment that has to fit an existing process.

  • Send the part detail, drawing, photograph or sizes
  • We quote against it
  • Build, finish and deliver on the agreed date
