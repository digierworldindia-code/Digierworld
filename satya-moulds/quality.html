Inspection Capability

Inspection

Inspection Within the Manufacturing Setup

Mould and die components are measured against the drawing during machining and before assembly.

Inspection is supported by an ATQ Vision Measuring Machine with a 200 × 200 × 200 mm measurement size and a range of dimensional instruments.

  • Vision Measuring Machine
  • Vernier calipers
  • Height gauges
  • Bore gauges
  • Slip gauges
  • Pin gauges
  • Low-force vernier calipers
