Approach

Designed Around Your Production Requirement

Every preform application has its own production requirements.

Our approach begins with understanding the component, tooling requirement and intended production process before developing the mould.

Tooling

Our PET Preform Tooling

PET preform moulds are one of the core tooling categories at Satya Moulds, developed with the same design, machining and inspection setup that supports our plastic injection mould work.

Tooling specifications are worked out for each project against the customer's preform drawing and production requirement, and confirmed with the customer before manufacturing begins.

What helps us respond to a PET preform enquiry

  • Preform drawing, CAD data or a physical sample
  • Intended application and bottle type
  • Expected production requirement
  • Details of the machine the mould will run on, where available
  • Existing mould details, if the requirement is a replacement or modification
