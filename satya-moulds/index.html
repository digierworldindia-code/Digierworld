About Satya Moulds

Engineering Mould Solutions Built Around Your Requirements

Established in 2013 under the leadership of Mr. Ranbeer Jangid (M.D.), Satya Moulds has developed expertise in mould making and tooling for automotive, electrical and medical component applications.

Our approach is simple: understand the requirement, develop the right tooling solution and manufacture it with the precision required for reliable production.

With experienced engineers, skilled manufacturing personnel and a dedicated machining and inspection setup, Satya Moulds works to deliver dependable tooling solutions for its customers.
